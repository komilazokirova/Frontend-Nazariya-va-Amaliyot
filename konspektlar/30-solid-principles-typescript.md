# 🏛️ TypeScriptda SOLID Tamoyillari

1. **S** — Single Responsibility Principle (Yagona javobgarlik).
2. **O** — Open/Closed Principle (Kengayishga ochiq, o'zgartirishga yopiq).
3. **L** — Liskov Substitution Principle (Voris klass ota klass o'rnini to'liq bosa olishi kerak).
4. **I** — Interface Segregation Principle (Katta interfeyslarni kichik va maxsus interfeyslarga bo'lish).
5. **D** — Dependency Inversion Principle (Yuqori modullar quyi modullarga emas, abstraksiyaga tayanishi kerak).
